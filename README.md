## Description 
Event Crew is On-demand event staff, setup, and volunteer application, matching people who are plannig an event to volunteers and service providors, who can help the planner with setup and other event needs. Providers can create profiles, advertise their services, manage their work history, and interact with customer reviews. The goal is to provide aconvenient and reliable way to find help for their events while giving providers a platform to offer their services.

## App Functions

1. Customer (Justin Highland):
    1. Create customer account - Create an account and provide information needed to book services.
    2. Browse available agencies - Browse available providers and services, and their ratings.
    3. Write reviews - Leave feedback on providers and services. 
    4. Book services - Book an event crew for an event.

2. Provider
    1. Create/modify/remove provider profile - create/update/delete a profile containing information about themselves, their experience, and the event services they provide.
    2. Create services - Providers will be able to create and manage listings for the event services they offer.
    3. View customer statistics - Providers will be able to view information about their customers and booked services.
    4. Reply to reviews - Providers will be able to view customer reviews of their services and respond to those reviews.
